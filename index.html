About KiranaGo

KiranaGo is a booking website for kirana and retail shop owners in Bhubaneswar, Odisha. Pick up goods from the wholesale market, book an auto, tempo or small truck in one tap, and pay the driver after delivery. Drivers with an auto, tempo or small truck can join to get new wholesale delivery jobs near them.

Terms of use

KiranaGo connects shop owners with drivers. The fare shown is for the trip and is paid to the driver after delivery. Please check your goods at delivery. KiranaGo is not responsible for damage or disputes between users.

Privacy

We keep your email, name, mobile number and booking details only to run the service. A shop and a driver see each other's name and phone only for an accepted booking. We do not sell your data.
